# Markdown Printer for iPhone and iPad 1.1

Markdown Printer now feels at home on iPad.

- Read in portrait, landscape, or a resizable window with a comfortable reading column.
- Keep separate documents open in independent windows, each with its own navigation, search, and reading position.
- Open linked Markdown in the current window or choose Open in New Window.
- Work with keyboard shortcuts for opening documents, finding text, sharing PDFs, printing, and managing windows.
- Save, share, and print through native iPad controls that adapt as you rotate or resize.
- Open Markdown shared from other apps more reliably on the latest iOS.

Your Markdown stays read-only, and PDF generation happens on your device.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Version 1.1, build 2.
