# Markdown Printer 1.4.8

This update repairs PDF and Word export by holding and dragging the document preview.

Click and hold the preview to pick up the document. The miniature now shows the document content, white paper, page outline, and format badge together, before you move the mouse. Drag it to Finder or another app to export in your preferred format; hold Option to switch between PDF and Word.

Ordinary clicks, text selection, links, and Find remain available in the preview.

This universal macOS 14+ build supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs and is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
