# Markdown Printer 1.4.6

This update adds more control over footers and exports, makes multiple documents easier to work with, and fixes several everyday frustrations with Find, recent files, and PDF footnotes.

## More Control Over Your Output

The new **Date & Time with Time Zone** footer option adds a compact timezone label, such as EDT or EST, to the file's last-modified date and time. Choose it for either footer; it follows your regional date/time format and uses the timezone offset appropriate to the file's timestamp.

You can now change your mind while dragging an export. Press or release **Option** during the drag to switch between PDF and Word. The thumbnail badge and the file you drop update together, without changing your saved export preference.

## A Smoother Document Workspace

New document windows now open in a staggered arrangement that keeps their title bars visible, making it easier to move between several documents. Restored window positions and tab groups are preserved.

Finder Quick Look now shows unavailable images as neat bordered boxes with their filenames. This makes missing images easier to identify without exposing full file paths; Quick Look remains offline.

## Everyday Fixes

- **Paste into Find:** Command-V and the standard editing commands work again in the Find field, including replacing a selected search query.
- **Reliable PDF footnotes:** Footnote numbers jump to their notes, and return links lead back to the first reference, including after saving and reopening the PDF.
- **Open Recent stays available:** Fixes the recent-files submenu disappearing when the app updates its menus.

This universal macOS 14+ build supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs and is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
