# Markdown Printer 1.4.5

This update improves Find, makes switching between PDF and Word exports easier, and refines document typography and pagination.

## Find Controls Stay Within Reach

- Fixes the Find panel's initial sizing and placement so its controls are visible.
- Pressing **Command-F** brings an off-screen Find panel back into view while preserving your search query and selected match.

## Quickly Switch Export Formats

- Hold **Option** while dragging from the preview or using **Share** to export Word when your default is PDF, or PDF when your default is Word, for that action only.
- Ordinary drags and Share actions continue to use your saved export preference.
- Both formats show a first-page thumbnail with a clear **PDF** or **Word** badge during the initial hold and drag.

## Clearer Headings and Better Page Breaks

- Gives all six heading levels a clearer hierarchy of sizes, weights, and spacing across PDF preview, saving, printing, Word export, and Finder Quick Look.
- Keeps headings visually connected to the content that follows them without extra blank lines.
- Fixes unnecessary page breaks in compact outlines when moving a heading group also pushed earlier body text forward.

## Release History in the App

- Open **Help > Release Notes**, or use the **Release Notes** link in **About Markdown Printer**, to see the latest published notes and earlier releases.

This universal macOS 14+ build supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs and is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
