# Markdown Printer Privacy Policy

Effective: August 27, 2026

Markdown Printer is a local-first Markdown reader and PDF creator for iPhone and Mac.

## Data collection

Markdown Printer does not collect personal data, document contents, analytics, usage data, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data. It does not use tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs.

## Documents and files

Documents you choose are read and processed on your device. Markdown Printer does not upload document contents. Remote images referenced by a Markdown document are not downloaded; the app shows a placeholder instead.

On iPhone, the app stores security-scoped folder permissions that you explicitly grant so linked local documents and images can open again. Those permissions stay on the device in the app's local preferences.

## User-directed sharing and links

When you choose Share, Export, Print, or an external link, Apple-provided system services or the destination you select may receive the item you directed the app to send or open. Their privacy policies govern those services. Markdown Printer does not perform those transfers without your action.

## Support

For questions or privacy requests, use the [Markdown Printer support page](ios-support.md) or [open an issue](https://github.com/pedstrom/md-printer/issues).

Material changes to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
